01 — THE CODEX

Sacred decrees

Five laws keep the hearth warm. Learn them before your first quest — every rank is earned on top of them.
  1. I

    Honour the Party

    Respect every member, in chat and in-game. No harassment, slurs or personal attacks.

  2. II

    Answer the Call

    If you RSVP to a quest, show up. Can't make it? Release your slot 24 hours ahead.

  3. III

    Share the Rupees

    Loot, tips and routes belong to the guild. Teach freely, hoard nothing.

  4. IV

    Keep the Hearth Clean

    No spoilers outside tagged channels. No spam, no self-promo without Council blessing.

  5. V

    Heed the Council

    Officers keep the peace. Disputes go to #council-hall, never to public channels.

02 — THE ORDER

Six ranks, one ascent

From Kokiri sapling to Hero of Time. Promotions are reviewed by the Council at the end of every moon.

Every hero begins with a song.

  1. I

    Kokiri Initiate

    Join the Discord and introduce yourself

    Access to #tavern and newcomer quests

  2. II

    Deku Scout

    Attend 3 guild events

    RSVP to raids · voice channel access

  3. III

    Hylian Soldier

    10 events + one completed dungeon run

    Tournament entry · guide drafts

  4. IV

    Knight of Hyrule

    Lead an event or publish a guide

    Event hosting · #knights-quarters

  5. V

    Sage of the Temple

    Appointed by the Council

    Officer tools · moderation · voting rights

  6. VI

    Hero of Time

    Guild Master — one per age

    Keeper of the Triforce, final word on all decrees
